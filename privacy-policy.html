Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version

  • We only collect what we need: usually your name and email address.
  • We use it to send you the checklist and, if you said yes, occasional emails.
  • We never sell or rent your information.
  • You can unsubscribe, see your information, or ask us to delete it at any time.

Lifinity Financial Services ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, how we handle personal information collected through lifinity.ca. We follow the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

1. What we collect

  • When you download the checklist: your first name, email address, whether you agreed to receive emails, and the date and time you agreed.
  • When you book a call: the details you enter in the booking form, such as your name, email, phone number and a note about what you'd like to discuss.
  • When you email or call us: whatever you choose to share with us.
  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may record standard information such as your IP address and browser type to keep the site secure and running.

This website does not ask for your SIN, banking details or financial account information. Please don't send these by email or through the website.

2. Why we collect it

  • To send you the checklist you requested.
  • To send you occasional emails with financial tips and updates, only if you checked the consent box.
  • To schedule and prepare for a call you booked.
  • To reply to your questions.

We will not use your information for any other purpose without asking you first, unless the law requires it.

3. Your consent

By submitting a form on this site, you agree to us using your information for the purpose described on that form. The email consent box is always unchecked to start with: we only add you to our mailing list if you tick it yourself.

You can change your mind at any time. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, or you can email gmon@lifinity.ca and we'll remove you.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information. We use a small number of trusted service providers to run this website and our communications:

  • Brevo — sends our emails and stores our mailing list.
  • Zoom — runs our appointment booking and video calls.
  • Cloudflare — hosts this website.

These providers may store information outside Canada (for example, in the United States or the European Union), where it may be subject to the laws of that country. We choose providers that protect information to a standard comparable to Canadian law, and they may only use your information to provide their service to us.

If you later become a client, the information needed for any application or policy is handled under separate client privacy notices, including those of our managing general agency and the insurance companies involved.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above. If you unsubscribe, we keep a record of that request so we don't email you again. If you ask us to delete your information, we will do so unless we are legally required to keep it.

6. How we protect it

We use secure, reputable services, strong passwords and two-factor authentication, and we limit access to your information to the people who need it. No method of storing or sending information online is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

7. Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies. Our hosting provider and the signup form may use small, essential cookies to keep the site secure and to make the form work. You can block cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that's wrong;
  • withdraw your consent and unsubscribe at any time;
  • ask us to delete your information.

Just email us. We will respond within 30 days.
