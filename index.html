Questions

What people ask before they book.

Something else on your mind? Email gmon@lifinity.ca.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. The discovery call is for understanding your situation. You'll only see recommendations at a second meeting, and the decision is always yours.

How is the advisor paid?

Mostly through commissions from the insurance companies whose products are recommended. You hear exactly how it works, including any conflicts of interest, at the start of your first call.

Which provinces does Lifinity serve?

Nova Scotia, Ontario, Alberta, British Columbia and Saskatchewan. If you live somewhere else, Lifinity can't advise you yet.

Why a laptop or desktop?

You'll look at numbers and illustrations together on screen. A phone screen is too small to follow along comfortably.

What should I have ready?

Nothing for the first call. If you already have policies or a group benefits booklet from work, keep them nearby.

Is Lifinity a fit for newcomers?

Yes. Lifinity's founder came to Canada as a student. Some insurance options depend on your residency status, and that gets checked together on your first call.

Does Lifinity manage investment portfolios?

Not yet. Today, Lifinity's advice covers life and health insurance, including segregated funds. Investment services are in the works, and your advisor is working toward the licensing needed to offer them. Until then, stocks, ETFs and mutual funds aren't offered.
