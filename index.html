What's inside

Everything you need, on a few clear pages.

No jargon and no sales pitch — just the steps to file with confidence and make a smart RRSP decision for your family.

  1. Your real contribution room

    Where to find your RRSP limit on your CRA Notice of Assessment or My Account, and how unused room carries forward year to year.

  2. RRSP, TFSA or FHSA — which first?

    A simple way to decide where your next dollar should go, based on your income today and the goals you're saving for.

  3. The documents to gather

    T4s and other slips, childcare, tuition, medical and moving receipts — plus a section for newcomers filing in Canada for the first time.

  4. The dates that matter

    RRSP contributions by March 1, 2027. Tax returns and any balance owing by April 30, 2027. Self-employed filing by June 15, 2027.
